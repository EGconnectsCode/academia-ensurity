⛔ Accidental Death Benefit — ExclusionsBeneficio por Muerte Accidental — Exclusiones

No benefit for accidental death is payable if the Insured's death is caused or contributed to by: disease or infirmity of mind or body, or medical or surgical treatment for such disease or infirmity; an infection not occurring as a direct result or consequence of an accidental bodily injury; any attempt at suicide, or intentional self-inflicted injury, while sane or insane; travel in an aircraft or device used for testing or experimental purposes, used by or for military authority or used for travel beyond the earth's atmosphere; active participation in a riot or insurrection; committing or attempting to commit a felony; intoxication as defined by the jurisdiction where the accidental injury occurred; riding or driving an air, land or water vehicle in a race, speed or endurance contest; rock or mountain climbing; bungee jumping; or aeronautics (hang-gliding, skydiving, parachuting, ultralight, soaring, ballooning and parasailing).

No benefit for accidental death is payable if the Insured's death is caused or materially contributed to by: voluntary intake or use by any means of (a) any drug, unless prescribed or administered by a physician and taken in accordance with the physician's instructions, or (b) poison, gas or fumes, unless a direct result of an occupational accident; or participation in an illegal occupation or activity; or if death occurs while the Insured is incarcerated.No se pagará ningún beneficio por muerte accidental si la muerte del Asegurado es causada o contribuida por: enfermedad o debilidad de mente o cuerpo, o tratamiento médico o quirúrgico; infección no resultante directamente de una lesión corporal accidental; intento de suicidio o lesión autoinfligida intencional; viaje en aeronave experimental o militar; participación activa en disturbios o insurrección; comisión o intento de delito grave; intoxicación; manejo en competencia de velocidad; alpinismo; puenting; aeronáutica. Tampoco se pagará si la muerte es causada por: uso voluntario de drogas no prescritas, veneno, gas o vapores (salvo accidente ocupacional); participación en actividad u ocupación ilegal; o si el Asegurado fallece mientras está encarcelado.
